About TRIPURA PEOPLE

TRIPURA PEOPLE is a work and task marketplace platform. Customers can post tasks or service requirements, and workers can find suitable tasks and complete them.

Workers receive rewards according to completed and approved tasks, subject to the platform's terms and applicable verification.

How TRIPURA PEOPLE Works

1. Customer Posts a Task

A customer or business describes a genuine task or service requirement.

2. Worker Finds a Task

Workers can view available tasks and choose suitable work.

3. Work is Completed

The worker completes the required task according to the instructions.

4. Task is Reviewed

The submitted work can be reviewed before the reward is approved.

5. Reward

After approval, the applicable reward is added to the worker's account.

6. Withdrawal

Eligible users may request withdrawal through supported payment methods, subject to verification and applicable rules.

For Workers

  • Browse available tasks.
  • Choose work suitable for you.
  • Complete the actual task.
  • Submit your work for review.
  • Receive the applicable reward after approval.
  • Request withdrawal when eligible.

Rewards are for completed work. TRIPURA PEOPLE does not promise guaranteed income or guaranteed returns.

For Customers & Businesses

  • Post genuine tasks or service requirements.
  • Describe the work clearly.
  • Set the applicable task reward.
  • Review submitted work.
  • Communicate with workers when necessary.

Pricing & Platform Fees

Task rewards and applicable platform fees may vary depending on the task and service arrangement.

Any applicable fee will be displayed or communicated before the relevant transaction or task arrangement.

Withdrawal Policy

Users must meet the applicable withdrawal requirements before requesting a payout.

  • Minimum withdrawal limits may apply.
  • User/payment details may require verification.
  • Incorrect beneficiary information may cause a payout to fail.
  • Processing time may depend on verification and the payment provider.

TRIPURA PEOPLE does not guarantee a fixed payout time for every transaction.

Refund & Cancellation Policy

If a customer cancels a task before work begins, the applicable amount may be handled according to the task terms and platform policy.

If a dispute occurs regarding completed work, TRIPURA PEOPLE may review the available task information and submitted evidence before deciding the applicable platform action.

Terms & Conditions

  • Users must provide accurate information.
  • Users must not create fraudulent or misleading tasks.
  • Workers must complete genuine tasks according to instructions.
  • Fraudulent activity may result in account restrictions.
  • Users must comply with applicable laws.
  • Rewards are connected to actual task completion and approval.

Privacy Policy

TRIPURA PEOPLE may collect information necessary for account creation, task management, customer support, verification and payment processing.

Payment and payout information may be shared with authorised payment service providers when required to process a transaction.

TRIPURA PEOPLE will use reasonable measures to protect user information and will handle information according to applicable laws and its operational requirements.
